Emergency Toilet Repair in Trussville, AL

An overflowing toilet will not wait until morning. Call the 24/7 line, and shut the water off first using the steps below.

Call (205) 896-0120

Stop the water first, then call

A toilet that is rising toward the rim needs two quick actions. Lift the tank lid and push the flapper down, or reach behind the toilet and turn the small shutoff valve clockwise until it stops. Our line is open 24/7 and we book the earliest visit we can.

Do not keep flushing to see if it clears. Each flush sends more water into the bowl. Lay towels at the base, and keep children and pets out of the room until the area is dry.

What to do while you wait

These steps keep a bad morning from becoming a damaged floor.

  1. Close the shutoff valve behind the toilet, turning it clockwise.
  2. If the valve will not turn, close the main water valve for the house.
  3. Mop up standing water with towels, because water under flooring does damage quickly.
  4. Do not use a drain chemical in a full bowl, it can splash back as you plunge.

Why a toilet clog is sometimes bigger than the toilet

A single clogged toilet that clears with a plunger is a toilet problem. A toilet that clogs again and again, or one that gurgles when you run the shower, points to a partial blockage in the drain behind it.

If other fixtures slow down too, read our page on sewer line cleaning, because the main line may be the real issue. For a simpler single fixture, drain cleaning is usually enough.

When a toilet should be replaced

A toilet that has cracked in the bowl or tank cannot be repaired, and one that needs a new wax ring every year may have a bent flange or a rocking base. If the toilet is decades old, a modern model can use noticeably less water per flush.

We talk through the options before anything is ordered. If a new toilet fits your budget and your bathroom, we can talk through timing by phone, and we haul the old one away.

What we check after a leak at the base

Water at the base of a toilet can soak into the subfloor long before you see damage. After repairing the seal, we press the floor around the toilet for soft spots and look for stains around the flange.

If the floor feels spongy, we tell you plainly, because replacing a damaged subfloor is a different job from a plumbing repair. Catching it early keeps the cost down.

Water and the parts inside your tank

Trussville Gas and Water's 2026 report lists total hardness as 205, though the table does not print a unit. Mineral buildup is a common reason older fill valves and flappers stop sealing, in any town with limestone-fed groundwater.

If a repaired toilet keeps running, ask us to check the parts for scale, because a quick cleaning sometimes restores a seal that otherwise looks fine.

How much water a toilet should use

According to the EPA's WaterSense program, a labeled toilet uses no more than 1.28 gallons per flush. Older toilets can use several times that, so a replacement can lower the water bill.

If a toilet needs more than one flush to clear, check the flapper and fill level before assuming it needs replacing.
