Water Heater Installation in Trussville, AL

A new water heater is set up once and should run for years. Call to talk through size, fuel and venting before you buy.

Call (205) 896-0120

Start with the right size and fuel

The best tank is the one that matches how many people use hot water and how they use it at the same time. A household with two bathrooms and a dishwasher running in the morning needs more capacity than a pair of retirees.

Fuel matters too. Natural gas heaters recover faster and cost less to run in many homes, and electric models suit houses without a gas line. Trussville Gas and Water supplies natural gas in its service area, so ask the utility whether gas is available at your address.

Gas or electric tank

Both work well. The right choice depends on your house.

What happens on installation day

Here is the normal order of work, so nothing comes as a surprise.

  1. We shut off water, power or gas and drain the old tank.
  2. The old unit comes out and the space is checked for rot, rust or leaks.
  3. The new unit is set, connected, vented and fitted with new supply connections and a pressure relief valve.
  4. We fill, test for leaks and gas leaks where relevant, and show you the shutoffs and settings.

When to replace instead of repair

A tank that is leaking from the body cannot be repaired, because the steel has failed. A tank that is more than ten years old and needs a costly part is also a candidate for replacement.

If the unit is younger and the problem is a burner, element or thermostat, a repair may be the better value. We tell you which way the numbers point, and you decide.

Efficiency, space and what to ask your installer

Ask about the unit's recovery rate and warranty, and what it costs to run. A well-insulated tank loses less heat, and a drain pan with a drain line limits damage if the tank ever fails.

Also ask where the old unit's discharge pipe and venting run. Moving a heater even a few feet can mean new gas, vent or water lines, so pick the location with that in mind.

Safety details that are easy to overlook

A gas heater must vent combustion gases outside and have enough air to burn properly. A water heater also needs a pressure relief valve with a discharge pipe that runs to a safe spot, and many installers add an expansion tank and a drain pan where the situation calls for one.

If you smell gas at any time, leave the house and call 911 or the gas utility from outside. Do not turn on lights or use a phone inside. For a unit that already leaks, see water heater repair or replacement.

What affects water heater installation cost

Ask for a clear price before work starts. These factors set it.

Tank size

A larger tank costs more to buy and sometimes to set.

Fuel and venting

Gas installs can need flue or gas line work.

Location

Attics and tight closets take more time than a garage.

Code upgrades

An older setup may need a new valve, pan or venting.

Permit

Ask the building department at 205-655-5483 whether your job needs one.

Some utilities and manufacturers offer rebates on efficient models. Ask the utility and check the manufacturer's page.

Check who does the work

The Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board lets consumers search for licensees. It is a sensible step for any water heater job, especially one that touches a gas line.

For permit questions, City of Trussville Building Safety and Inspections is the office to call.

The standards a careful installer follows

Gas water heaters are installed to the manufacturer's instructions and the National Fuel Gas Code, NFPA 54. Plumbing codes such as the IPC and UPC cover the relief valve, its discharge pipe and the water connections.

Ask your installer which code edition your jurisdiction uses, and keep the permit paperwork if one was needed.
