About Trussville Express Plumbing in Trussville, AL

We are a 24/7 plumbing line based at 1423 Gadsden Hwy in Trussville. Here is how we work and what you can expect when you call.

Call (205) 896-0120

Who we are

Trussville Express Plumbing is a plumbing service based at 1423 Gadsden Hwy in Trussville, Alabama. Our phone line is open 24/7, and we book the earliest visit we can.

We focus on common household problems: clogged drains, leaks, water heaters, sewer lines and failed fixtures. Each has its own page on this site, written for Trussville homes and businesses.

How a call works

You describe what you see. We tell you what to shut off, such as the main water valve or power to a wet room, and we book the earliest visit we can.

On arrival, we look at the problem before we quote. For hidden leaks and sewer lines that means equipment, such as listening gear and cameras, so we can show you what is wrong instead of asking you to take our word for it.

How we work on a repair

The sequence is the same whether the job is a faucet or a sewer line.

  1. Find the cause, with equipment where the problem is hidden.
  2. Explain the options in plain language, including the cost of waiting.
  3. Ask for a clear price before work starts.
  4. Do the work, test it and show you what changed.

How we price

Ask for a clear price before any work starts. Our service pages list what drives the cost of each repair, so you know what to expect before you call.

We do not quote prices on this site, because a fair price depends on your house, your pipes and the time of day. We do not promise arrival times either. We tell you when we can be there, and we say so plainly if that changes.

What we will tell you honestly

If a repair will not hold, we say so and explain the options. If a problem is the utility's, such as a water main in the street, we point you to the utility. If a job needs a permit, we tell you to check with the building department.

We also say when a do-it-yourself fix is reasonable. A running toilet flapper or a clogged aerator does not need a plumber, and our blog explains how to handle them.

Where we work

Dispatch covers Trussville and the nearby towns of Clay, Pinson, Leeds and Springville. Our service area page has the details.

Trussville is in Jefferson County, with a small part in St. Clair County. Local facts such as the water supply, the age of housing and the permit office show up in our pages because they change how we diagnose problems.

Check any plumber before you hire

The Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board offers a licensee search for consumers on its website. It is a good habit for any plumber you hire, including us.

For permit questions on larger work, the City of Trussville Department of Building Safety and Inspections is at 205-655-5483. Ask before work starts.

What we do

Our plumbing services include drain cleaning, water heater repair, leak detection, sewer line repair, repiping and commercial plumbing.

Each page explains the signs to watch for, what to do while you wait and how the repair works. If you would like to read more before calling, the blog goes deeper on costs and tests.

Why we keep the website simple

Plumbing problems are urgent, so the site is built to get you to a phone call quickly. Every page has the number at the top, and a call bar stays on screen on a phone.

We do not collect your details through forms, and we do not ask you to wait for a callback. You describe the problem to a person, and that person tells you what to do next.

What you can read here before you call

Our service pages explain each repair, from the signs to watch for to the factors that set the price. Our blog covers the questions people ask most, such as how to test for a hidden leak and when a clog needs a plumber.

Reading ahead does not replace a visit, but it helps you ask better questions. If something on a page does not match what you see at home, call and tell us.

Contact

The fastest way to reach us is the phone, at (205) 896-0120. We do not use web forms, so there is nothing to fill out and no one will call you back unexpectedly.

You can also write to contact@expressplumbertrussville.com, though email is slower than a call when water is moving. Our contact page has the address and a map.
