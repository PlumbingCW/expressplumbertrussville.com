When a failed water heater cannot wait

A tank that is leaking steadily or has stopped heating entirely is a replacement call, not a repair call, and our 24/7 line is open nights and weekends for it. We book the earliest visit we can.

Start with these steps while you wait:

  1. Close the cold water valve on top of the tank. That stops new water from feeding the leak.
  2. Turn off power to an electric unit at the breaker, or set a gas unit's control to off. If you smell gas, leave and call 911 and your gas utility first.
  3. Drain a few gallons through the hose bib at the bottom only if the leak is heavy and the floor drain is nearby. Never open hot taps while the heater is off and the tank is empty.
  4. Take photos of the tank, the label and any water damage before you clean up. Your insurer may ask for them.

What a replacement actually involves

A swap is not just lifting one tank out and another in. The new unit has to match the fuel, the venting, the gas or electrical supply and the space, and the old fittings often need new connectors, a new relief valve discharge pipe and a drain pan where one is required.

We confirm the details on the phone first so the right unit is on the truck. Then we drain and disconnect the old tank, set the new one, restore water and fuel, check the venting and test the relief valve. The old unit leaves with us.

Why age and water quality matter here

Many Trussville homes date to the 1980s boom that followed the completion of I-59 and I-459, which means original tanks from that era are long gone and second or third replacements are common. A tank is often a decade old when it fails, and sooner where mineral scale builds up. Owners in an established subdivision such as Stonegate can use our home-age checklist.

The utility's 2026 water quality report lists total hardness as 205 with no unit printed in the table. Since the number is ambiguous, test your own water, and flush any new tank every year so sediment does not shorten its life.

What affects the cost of a water heater replacement

Ask for a clear price before work starts. These are the factors that set it.

Tank size and type

A 40 gallon tank, a 50 gallon tank and a hybrid unit are three different price points.

Fuel and venting

Gas units need proper venting, and a changed vent path adds work.

Code upgrades

Expansion tanks, drain pans and discharge piping may be needed to meet current requirements. Ask the Trussville building department about permit rules, since they vary.

Access and haul-away

Attics and tight closets take longer, and removing the old unit is part of the visit.

Emergency timing

Night and weekend installs are priced as emergency work before you agree.

A new tank is not covered by homeowners insurance as a rule, though damage from a sudden failure sometimes is. Keep the receipt and the photos.

Standards that apply to a replacement

Gas appliance installation follows the manufacturer's instructions and the National Fuel Gas Code, NFPA 54. Plumbing codes such as the IPC and UPC also call for a temperature and pressure relief valve with a discharge pipe.

Those are the standards a careful installer works to, and the tank's label and manual list the specifics for your model.
