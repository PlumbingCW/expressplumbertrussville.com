Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026.

What this site collects

This website has no forms, accounts or comment fields, so it does not collect personal information through the pages themselves. We do not sell personal information.

Our web host and the third-party services used by the site, such as Google Fonts and Google Maps embeds, may receive standard technical data like your IP address and browser type when a page loads.

When you call us

When you call our phone line, we may collect the number you call from and the details you give us about your plumbing problem. We use them to book and complete service.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you prefer not to be recorded, tell us at the start of the call.

How we use information

We use call information to schedule visits, give quotes, complete work and keep records of service. We may share information with contractors working on your job and with others when the law requires it.

We keep information only as long as we need it for those purposes or as the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to legal and record-keeping duties. Write to contact@expressplumbertrussville.com.

Your browser settings control cookies and similar tools used by third-party services. This site does not set its own advertising cookies.

Changes

We may update this policy, and the date at the top will change when we do. Questions can go to contact@expressplumbertrussville.com or to our address at 1423 Gadsden Hwy, Trussville, AL 35173.

See also our terms of service.
