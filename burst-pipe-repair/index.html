When a pipe bursts: the first ten minutes

A burst pipe is the clearest plumbing emergency there is. Our line is open 24/7, and we book the earliest visit we can. What you do in the first minutes limits the damage more than anything that happens after.

Follow these steps in order:

  1. Find the main shutoff valve and turn it clockwise until it stops. In many homes it sits near the meter, the water heater or where the line enters the house.
  2. Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain what is left in the pipes.
  3. Turn off power to any wet area at the breaker only if you can reach the panel without standing in water. Otherwise stay out and call.
  4. Move furniture and valuables, take photos for your insurer, and start removing standing water with towels or a wet vacuum.

Why pipes burst in this part of Alabama

A hard freeze is the classic cause, and north Jefferson County sees them often enough that exposed pipes in garages, crawl spaces and outside walls are worth wrapping before winter. Freezing is only one cause, though. High water pressure, aging galvanized steel and corroded fittings split pipes in July as easily as in January.

Trussville's older streets near Chalkville Road date to the 1930s and 40s, and homes from that era were commonly plumbed in galvanized steel that rusts from the inside. If your house has it, a burst is more a question of when than whether, and a repipe is worth pricing.

We repair the pipe. Drying walls and replacing drywall is a separate restoration trade, and we will tell you when it is time to call one.

After the shutoff: what comes next

Once the water is off, we find the failed section, check the pipes on either side for the same weakness and repair or replace what we find. A pipe that burst once often has neighbors in the same condition, and fixing one section while leaving a rusted run beside it just moves the problem.

If the break is under a slab, see slab leak repair. If it is in the yard between the meter and the house, that is a water service line repair. Either way, you will know the plan and the price before we begin.

What affects the cost of a burst pipe repair

Ask for a clear price before work starts. These are the factors that decide it.

Pipe material

Copper, PEX and galvanized steel each need different fittings and time.

Location

A pipe in an open crawl space is far easier than one behind finished walls.

Extent of damage

One split is a short repair. A run of corroded pipe may be better replaced.

Water cleanup

We stop and repair the leak. Drying and rebuilding are billed by the restoration company.

Time of the call

Night, weekend and holiday calls are quoted as emergency work.

Homeowners policies commonly cover sudden pipe bursts and the resulting damage, though not the pipe itself or gradual leaks. Call your insurer early and keep photos.

Pipe materials and the standards behind them

Replacement supply pipe such as PEX and copper is manufactured to ASTM standards, and plumbing codes such as the IPC and UPC list which materials are approved for potable water.

Ask what material a repair uses and whether it matches the rest of the line, because mixing metals can cause corrosion at the joint.
