When a main line backup is an emergency

Every drain in the house running slow, the lowest tub or floor drain bubbling when you flush, or wastewater coming up anywhere means the main sewer line is blocked. Our line is open 24/7 and we book the earliest visit we can.

Do this while you wait:

  1. Stop using water in the house. Each flush or load of laundry adds to the backup.
  2. Find your cleanout, a capped pipe usually in the yard or basement, and leave the cap on. Do not open it yourself while the line is full.
  3. Keep people and pets away from the lowest drain, where wastewater tends to surface first.
  4. Photograph any water damage and note the time the backup started.

What the main line carries and where it blocks

Everything from every fixture ends up in one pipe that leaves the house and joins the public sewer. That single path is why a main line clog shows up everywhere at once, and why it tends to happen at the low points, belly sections and joints where debris settles.

In older Trussville neighborhoods the usual blockers are tree roots finding joints and grease building up on rough pipe walls. In newer homes it is often wipes, paper products and kitchen grease, which pass the first fixture and gather farther down.

Cleaning versus repairing

Cleaning helps only if the pipe is sound. If a camera shows a cracked, offset or collapsed section, cleaning gives short relief and the clog returns. In that case we say so and talk through sewer line repair instead of selling you another cleaning.

If the line is sound, a good cleaning followed by sensible habits, like keeping grease and wipes out of the drains, can last for years. For a quick comparison of the lighter option, see our drain cleaning page.

What affects the price of sewer line cleaning

Ask for a clear price before work starts. These factors set it.

Length of the line

A longer run takes more cable and more time.

What is in it

Grease, roots or a collapsed pipe each need different tools.

Cleanout access

A good cleanout makes the job easier. Without one we may need to remove a toilet to get in.

Camera add-on

Seeing the pipe adds to the visit and often saves money later.

Timing

Night and weekend backups are quoted as emergency work.

Insurance often does not cover cleaning, and sewer backup coverage depends on an endorsement. Ask your agent before you begin cleanup.

Check with the utility if neighbors are affected

Trussville Gas and Water publishes sewer information on its website. If neighbors on your street have backups at the same time, the problem may be in the public line, and the utility needs to hear about it.

If only your house is affected, the blockage is almost certainly in your own line, and a plumber can confirm that with a quick check at the cleanout.

Cleanouts and the plumbing code

Plumbing codes such as the IPC and UPC call for cleanouts at set points in a building drain so the line can be cleared without pulling a fixture. A good cleanout is the reason a cleaning visit is quick.

If your house has none, we can discuss adding one, and the building department can tell you what your jurisdiction requires.
