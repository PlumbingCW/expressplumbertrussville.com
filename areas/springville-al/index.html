Springville, one of the older towns in the area

Springville was incorporated in December 1880, and the 2020 census counted 4,786 residents. It sits in St. Clair County at about 735 feet above sea level, so it is a small town with long-standing homes.

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for Springville. Because it is a smaller community northeast of our Trussville office, a quick call helps us plan the route and the parts to bring.

Springville's own water supply

Springville's 2023 water report says the town buys water from Coosa Valley Water and Trussville Utilities, and also uses a Knox aquifer spring. It lists hardness at 125 ppm and pH 7.2, with chlorination for treatment.

Water that is moderately hard can still leave scale on heaters and faucet aerators over time. Call the Springville Water Department at 205-467-2704 for anything on the public side, and call us for the pipes inside your property.

Hot water in a small town

A water heater that fails on a cold morning is among the most common urgent calls anywhere. A tank leaking from the body needs replacement, while a pilot, thermostat or element problem is often repairable.

If you are deciding between the two, read water heater replacement for the signs of a tank at the end of its life. Unusual models can take extra time to source, so tell us the brand when you call.

Scale on aerators and heaters

Hardness of 125 ppm, as the town's report lists, is moderate, and minerals can still build up on faucet aerators and inside a tank. Sediment makes a heater noisy and less efficient over time.

Flushing a tank once a year helps, and unscrewing a clogged aerator to rinse it takes a minute. If a faucet still runs weak, our faucet repair page explains what we check next.

What to tell us when you call from Springville

Give the street address and say whether you are in town or on a county road, because that helps us plan the route. Describe what you see, such as water on the floor, no hot water or a drain that will not clear, and say whether the main valve is already closed.

If the problem is a water heater, tell us the brand and whether it runs on gas or electricity. If you can read the sticker on the tank, add the age. These details let us bring the right parts the first time.
