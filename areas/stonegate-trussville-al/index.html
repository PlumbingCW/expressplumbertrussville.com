If your water heater is more than ten years old

Tank water heaters do not last forever, and a failure usually happens at an inconvenient time.

  1. Find the data label on the tank and read the manufacture date or serial number.
  2. Look for rust, moisture or a stain on the floor at the base of the tank.
  3. Make sure you know where the cold water shutoff above the tank is and that it turns.
  4. If the tank is leaking from the body, turn off its water and power or gas, then call.
  5. Ask us about water heater replacement before the tank fails, so you can plan the cost.
