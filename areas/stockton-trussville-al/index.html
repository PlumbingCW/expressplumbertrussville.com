If you smell gas, leave first

A gas smell is never a plumbing call to make first. Some homes in newer communities use gas for cooking or hot water, and Stockton's 2017 model homes included a gas cooktop.

  • 1Get everyone out of the house right away.
  • 2Do not flip switches, use a phone inside or light anything.
  • 3From outside, call 911 and your gas utility.
  • 4Do not go back in until the utility or fire department says it is safe.
  • 5After the all clear, call us for any plumbing problem tied to the repair.

What Stockton is

Stockton is a community in Trussville developed by Signature Homes. A Trussville Tribune report from 2017 named its sections as The Preserve at Stockton, Stockton Cove and the Knoll, and said about 400 people lived there, with 260 homes sold in the first phase over four years.

A BhamNow article from 2023 described the community as in its final phase of building, with miles of walking paths, several pools, a gym and courts. A local real estate guide describes Stockton as newer construction with larger homes.

Larger homes put more fixtures on the same supply, so hot water and water bills are natural questions for owners of newer, bigger houses.

Hot water in a bigger home

If the hot water runs out before everyone has finished, a few facts help us diagnose it.

  1. Note how many people use hot water and at what times of day.
  2. Find out whether the water heater is a tank or a tankless unit, and whether it runs on gas or electricity.
  3. Check whether the problem is at every fixture or only one.
  4. Look at the label for the model number and age.
  5. Call us with these details, and read about water heater repair while you wait.
