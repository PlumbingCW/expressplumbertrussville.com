Trussville water and housing, and what they mean for your pipes

Trussville Gas and Water draws its supply from eight groundwater wells in limestone and chert aquifers, and, according to its 2026 report, total hardness is 205, though the table prints no unit. Mineral-rich water can build scale inside water heaters and on faucet aerators, so a heater that is slow to heat or a faucet with weak flow is often a scale problem first.

The city's housing runs from the Chalkville Road neighborhoods of the 1930s and 40s and the 1938 Cahaba Project, which brought indoor plumbing to 287 units, to subdivisions from the 1980s growth around I-59 and I-459. Pipe materials differ across those eras, so the first thing we ask on a call is roughly when your house was built.

What to do in a plumbing emergency before we arrive

Most plumbing emergencies are made smaller by the first five minutes. These steps apply to burst pipes, active leaks and overflowing fixtures.

  1. Close the main water valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house.
  2. Switch off power to wet rooms at the breaker panel if water is near outlets or appliances.
  3. Move people, pets and belongings away from the water and take photos for your records.
  4. Call our 24/7 emergency plumber line and tell us what you see, so we can book the right visit.
