How to choose where to start

Water where it should not be points to burst pipe repair or leak detection. Slow drains across the house point to sewer line cleaning, and one slow sink usually means drain cleaning.

No hot water or a leaking tank is a job for water heater repair or replacement. For a shop or restaurant, see commercial plumbing. If you are planning a remodel, start with plumbing installation.
