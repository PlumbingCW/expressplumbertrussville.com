Emergency Water Service Line Repair in Trussville, AL

The pipe from the meter to your house is out of sight until it fails. Call the 24/7 line if you have a soggy yard, low pressure or no water.

Call (205) 896-0120

When the water line fails

Water bubbling up in the yard, a sudden drop in pressure, no water at all, or a meter that spins when every tap is off point to a break in the service line. Our line is open 24/7 and we book the earliest visit we can.

Before we arrive:

  1. Close the shutoff valve at the house if you can find it. If the leak is at the meter side, the utility controls the meter valve.
  2. Keep people and vehicles off soft, flooded ground. A leak can undermine the soil.
  3. Note where the water is surfacing and photograph it.
  4. Check whether neighbors have low pressure too, which points to a utility problem.

What the service line is

The service line runs from the water meter to the house. In Trussville, water comes from Trussville Gas and Water, which draws on eight groundwater wells according to its 2026 water quality report. Past the meter, the pipe belongs to the homeowner and runs underground, often for a long stretch.

Older homes may have galvanized or copper lines, newer ones typically have plastic. Whatever you have, ask your utility where its responsibility ends and yours begins, because the boundary matters for who pays.

Repair or replace the line

The condition of the pipe and how it fails decide the answer.

If you are not sure the leak is on the line, leak detection confirms it before anyone digs.

Lead, copper and your own pipes

The utility's 2026 water quality report lists lead at 1 ppb and copper at 0.28 ppm at the 90th percentile from sampling in 2023, with testing every three years. Those numbers describe a sample of homes across the system and not your pipes.

If your service line or interior plumbing is old, ask us what material it is. Replacing an aging line is a sensible time to check, and a full repipe may be worth pricing if the house has old galvanized pipe inside as well.

What affects the cost of service line work

Ask for a clear price before work starts. Underground work is priced by these factors.

Length of the line

Meters set back from the house mean more pipe and more digging.

Depth and soil

Rocky or hard soil slows the dig.

Surface in the way

A lawn is easy to repair, but a driveway or walk adds cost.

Pipe type

The new pipe material and fittings affect the price.

Timing

Night and weekend calls are quoted as emergency work.

Some insurers offer service line coverage as an add-on, and standard policies often exclude it. Check yours.
