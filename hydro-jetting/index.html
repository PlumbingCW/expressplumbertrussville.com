Emergency Hydro Jetting in Trussville, AL

A cable punches a hole through a clog. A jetter cleans the whole pipe wall. Call the 24/7 line if a repeat backup has you tired of temporary fixes.

When a repeat backup calls for jetting

A line that clogs again every few weeks, a restaurant or home kitchen with constant grease trouble, or a main line backing up right now are the situations where jetting earns its cost. Our line is open 24/7 and we book the earliest visit we can.

While you wait for a visit:

  1. Stop sending water down the affected drains.
  2. Keep grease, wipes and food scraps out of the system until the line is clean.
  3. Note which fixtures back up first, because that points to where the blockage sits.
  4. If water is already rising, keep everyone away from the affected area and call right away.

How hydro jetting works

A jetter pumps water through a hose fitted with a nozzle that fires jets forward and backward. The forward jets break through a clog and the rear jets pull the hose along the pipe while they scrub the walls. The result is a full-width clean, not just a channel through the middle.

Pressure and nozzle choice matter. Using too much on a fragile old pipe risks damage, so we match the nozzle and pressure to the pipe material and condition, and we look with a camera when the line's condition is unknown.

Jetting compared with cabling

Both have their place. Here is how they differ.

Cable machineHydro jetting
Best atSoft clogs and quick fixesGrease, scale and root growth on pipe walls.
Leaves behindA narrow channelA clean full-bore pipe.
Typical useSingle sink, tub or short runMain lines, kitchen lines and repeat clogs.
RiskCan snag on damaged pipeNot suited to cracked or collapsed pipe without a camera look first.

For the simpler approach on a minor clog, start with drain cleaning.

Where jetting makes the most sense

Kitchen lines in busy households and commercial kitchens are the classic case, because grease builds in layers that a cable only punctures. Lines with root growth often respond too, though roots will regrow unless the pipe joint is repaired.

If a camera shows that the pipe itself is damaged, jetting will not hold, and a sewer line repair is the real answer. For restaurants and shops that need after-hours help, see commercial plumbing.

Getting ready for a jetting visit

A few minutes of preparation makes the job faster. Clear the area around the cleanout, move stored items away from the access point and make sure we can reach the lowest drain in the house. If the cleanout is outdoors, mark any sprinkler heads or fragile landscaping near it.

Tell us what you have already tried, including any store-bought drain chemicals. Chemicals left sitting in a line can splash back during cleaning, so we handle them with care and adjust the method to suit.

Older streets and mixed pipe materials

Trussville grew in stages, from the Chalkville Road neighborhoods of the 1930s and 40s to the I-59 and I-459 growth of the 1980s. That means one town can hold clay, cast iron and plastic drain pipe, sometimes on the same street.

Each material tolerates jetting differently, which is why we ask about the age of the house and look with a camera when the pipe is an unknown.

Pipe condition and the limits of pressure

Cast iron, clay and plastic drain pipe are each made to different standards and tolerate different pressures. That is why we look with a camera first when the material is unknown.

Always follow the pipe manufacturer's guidance and local code for work on a drain line, and ask us if you are unsure what you have.
