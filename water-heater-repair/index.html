When a water heater problem is an emergency

Water pooling around the tank, a rumbling or popping from inside it, or a scorched smell at the burner all call for quick action, and our line is open 24/7 for them. We book the earliest visit we can, including evenings and weekends.

While you wait for a plumber, do these in order:

  1. Smell gas? Leave the house, do not touch switches or phones inside, and call 911 and your gas utility before you call us. Trussville Gas and Water supplies natural gas in the city, and its emergency number is on your bill.
  2. If the tank is leaking, turn the cold water supply valve on top of the heater to the off position. For an electric unit, switch off its breaker too.
  3. Open a hot tap upstairs to relieve pressure if the relief valve is dripping steadily, and keep everyone away from the discharge pipe, because that water can be scalding.
  4. Put a bucket under a slow leak and move boxes off the floor so a small drip does not become a damaged room.

Why tanks fail faster than their warranty suggests

Trussville gets its water from eight groundwater wells drawing on the Tuscumbia-Fort Payne chert and Bangor limestone aquifers, according to the utility's own 2026 water quality report. The same report lists total hardness as 205, although the table does not print a unit, so treat it as a sign to test your own tap and not as a precise figure.

Water that has moved through limestone tends to leave mineral scale, and scale collects as sediment at the bottom of a tank. Sediment makes a gas burner work harder, makes an electric element run hot, and speeds up the corrosion that eventually opens a seam. That is why a yearly flush and a look at the anode rod add real years to a tank.

Common water heater problems and what usually fixes them

The symptom narrows the list quickly. This is how we read what you describe on the phone before we pick up a tool.

SymptomCommon causeTypical repair
No hot water, gas unitPilot out or failed thermocoupleRelight and replace the thermocouple.
No hot water, electric unitTripped breaker or burned-out elementReset the breaker, test and replace the element.
Lukewarm or runs out fastSediment, failed lower element or dip tubeFlush the tank, replace the part.
Popping or rumblingScale hardened on the tank floorFlush and descale, or plan a replacement.
Water around the baseLoose fitting, bad valve or a failed tankTighten or replace the fitting. A leaking tank means replacement.

A leak from the tank itself cannot be patched. If the water is coming through the steel, repair money is better spent on a new unit.

Gas, electric and hybrid units

A gas heater depends on the pilot or igniter, the thermocouple and the gas control valve. An electric heater depends on two elements, two thermostats and a high limit switch. A hybrid heat pump unit adds a compressor and fan, and it needs clear air space around it to work as designed.

We work on all three types. If your unit is more than ten years old and the repair bill is climbing, we will lay out the repair against the price of a replacement so you can choose, and we will mention a tankless unit when the layout makes sense for it.

What affects the cost of a water heater repair

Ask for a clear price before work starts. These factors decide how that price comes out.

The part

A thermocouple or element costs far less than a gas control valve or a tank.

Fuel type

Gas work includes safety checks on venting and gas connections that electric work does not.

Age of the unit

A tank past ten years may not be worth a large repair.

Access

A heater in a tight closet or attic takes more time than one in a garage.

Time of the call

Nights, weekends and holidays are emergency calls and are quoted that way.

Homeowners insurance often pays for sudden water damage from a failed tank but not for the heater itself or for wear. Photos taken before cleanup help if you file a claim.
